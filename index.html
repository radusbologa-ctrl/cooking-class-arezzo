What you'll do

From the welcome coffee to the last spoonful of tiramisù.

1

A warm Tuscan welcome

Meet your chef in Arezzo, enjoy a welcome coffee and tie on your apron — the fun starts immediately.

2

Make fresh pasta by hand

Flour, fresh eggs and your own hands: learn to mix, knead, roll and shape fresh pasta — silky pappardelle and ricotta-and-spinach ravioli — exactly as it's been done in Tuscany for generations.

3

Master authentic tiramisù

Real mascarpone cream, espresso-soaked savoiardi and a generous dusting of cocoa — Italy's most beloved dessert, from scratch.

4

Feast on what you made

Sit down to a full meal of your own dishes, paired with unlimited local Tuscan wines.

5

Take the recipes home

Leave with detailed recipes so you can recreate the taste of Tuscany in your own kitchen.

Frequently asked questions

What will I learn in the cooking class?

Fresh handmade pasta and authentic Italian tiramisù, the traditional Tuscan way, guided step by step by an expert chef. At the end you'll enjoy a full meal of everything you prepared, with unlimited local wines.

Do I need any cooking experience?

Not at all — the class is designed for everyone: complete beginners, families, couples and groups. Classes are kept small so the chef can give everyone personalized attention.

When do the classes take place?

Tuesday to Friday and on Sundays, with three time slots: 10:30 (€69), 13:00 (€79) and 17:30 (€89). Prior booking is required.

Can you accommodate vegetarians or allergies?

Yes, happily — just let us know about any dietary requirements when you book.

In which languages is the class taught?

English and Italian, with warm, clear, step-by-step explanations.

How do I get there?

The class takes place in Arezzo, Tuscany — walking distance from the train station, with plenty of parking nearby. We share the exact address, with precise arrival and parking directions, when you confirm your booking. Arezzo is about 30 minutes from Cortona and roughly an hour from Florence, Siena and Perugia.

How do I book?

Pick your time slot and tap "Reserve" to book via WhatsApp, or call +39 346 9776103 or email ilpoggiolinodoro@gmail.com. Booking in advance is required.

How long is the class? Do I need any experience?

The class lasts about 3 hours and is fully hands-on. No cooking experience is needed — you get step-by-step guidance in a small group, and the venue is wheelchair accessible. Verified travellers rate the experience 4.9/5, with 5/5 for the guide.

Is the class suitable for children and families?

Absolutely — families are some of our favourite guests. Groups of up to 9 are welcome, kids get set up with their own tools, and nobody minds a bit of flour on the floor. Vegetarian guests are happily accommodated too.

Where can I stay near the cooking class?

Many of our guests stay at Il Poggiolino d'Oro, a panoramic holiday home in the countryside 10 minutes from Arezzo — original wooden beams, valley views, up to 6 guests. Book the class and the stay together for the full Tuscan experience.

Can I combine the class with Arezzo's Christmas markets?

Yes! From mid-November to 6 January Arezzo hosts "Città del Natale", Tuscany's biggest Christmas market, with a Tyrolean village in Piazza Grande. Spend the morning at the markets and join the 13:00 or 17:30 class — then sleep in the countryside at Il Poggiolino d'Oro.

What else can I see around Arezzo?

Ponte Buriano — the Roman bridge many scholars recognise in the background of Leonardo da Vinci's Mona Lisa — is just minutes away. In town, don't miss Piero della Francesca's frescoes; Cortona, Siena and the Val d'Orcia make easy day trips.
