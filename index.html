What you'll do

From the welcome coffee to the last spoonful of tiramisù.

1

A warm Tuscan welcome

Meet your chef at Ristorante Le Corniole, enjoy a welcome coffee and tie on your apron — the fun starts immediately.

2

Make fresh pasta by hand

Flour, fresh eggs and your own hands: learn to mix, knead, roll and shape fresh pasta exactly as it's been done in Tuscany for generations.

3

Master authentic tiramisù

Real mascarpone cream, espresso-soaked savoiardi and a generous dusting of cocoa — Italy's most beloved dessert, from scratch.

4

Feast on what you made

Sit down to a full meal of your own dishes, paired with unlimited local Tuscan wines.

5

Take the recipes home

Leave with detailed recipes so you can recreate the taste of Tuscany in your own kitchen.

Frequently asked questions

What will I learn in the cooking class?

Fresh handmade pasta and authentic Italian tiramisù, the traditional Tuscan way, guided step by step by an expert chef. At the end you'll enjoy a full meal of everything you prepared, with unlimited local wines.

Do I need any cooking experience?

Not at all — the class is designed for everyone: complete beginners, families, couples and groups. Classes are kept small so the chef can give everyone personalized attention.

When do the classes take place?

Tuesday to Friday and on Sundays, with three time slots: 10:30 (€69), 13:00 (€79) and 17:30 (€89). Prior booking is required.

Can you accommodate vegetarians or allergies?

Yes, happily — just let us know about any dietary requirements when you book.

In which languages is the class taught?

English and Italian, with warm, clear, step-by-step explanations.

How do I get there?

Ristorante Le Corniole is at Viale Michelangelo 142, Arezzo — walking distance from the Arezzo train station, with plenty of parking nearby. Arezzo is about 30 minutes from Cortona and roughly an hour from Florence, Siena and Perugia.

How do I book?

Pick your time slot and tap "Reserve" to book via WhatsApp, or call +39 0575 1913733 or email ristorantelecorniole@gmail.com. Booking in advance is required.
